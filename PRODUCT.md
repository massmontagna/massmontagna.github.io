# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers in automotive and engineering-software roles. They arrive from LinkedIn, a CV, or a search, skim for a minute or less, and need to judge the profile fast: a vehicle-dynamics engineer who also writes real code. Secondary (not designed for separately): engineering peers and readers arriving from engineersnotebook.net.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Massimo Montagna. Success is a hiring visitor understanding the profile in seconds and opening LinkedIn.

## Positioning

The owner sits between mechanical engineering and software: Lead Vehicle Dynamics Analyst who builds Python/MATLAB models and automation for vehicle simulation. The hero's yaw-rate code snippet and its live chart (same formula, computed in the browser) show that directly rather than claim it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages at massmontagna.com. Hand-written HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build system.
- No third-party requests; fonts are self-hosted.
- Single page: hero, skills, current project, experience, contact.
- Content may be trimmed and restructured without changing facts; copy changes are shown to the owner before shipping.

## Brand Commitments

- Job title: "Mechanical Engineer / Data Analyst" (hero, title, og/twitter tags, README, og-image together).
- Current employer is never named: "a large automotive OEM" everywhere.
- No phone, email, CV download, or profile photo. Contact is LinkedIn and GitHub only.
- Experience stays a high-level overview; no detailed bullets.
- The yaw-rate code + chart in the hero stays (owner confirmed 2026-11-05). If the code text changes, the chart formula must change to match.
- Everything else of the current look (telemetry canvas, theme toggle, "M" mark) is open to replacement.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current project: Engineer's Notebook (https://engineersnotebook.net). No case-study pages or project images exist; do not fabricate them.
- Experience and skills copy in index.html, drawn from the owner's CV.
- No testimonials, metrics, or employer logos; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

- Prove, don't claim: real engineering shown working beats adjectives.
- Readable in under a minute by a non-specialist recruiter.
- Restraint over decoration; the owner finds the current site "vibe-codey".
