MOT Test Data Analysis

What makes a car fail its MOT? An analysis of 33 million UK car and van tests from 2024, using DVSA data, SQL in DuckDB and Python. Some findings:

  • 28.3% of cars fail first time. The failure rate rises from 12.4% at 3 years to a peak of 43.9% at 18, then falls as only well-kept cars survive.
  • Tyres are the most common failure on 3-year-old cars. By the time a car is about 8 years old, suspension, lighting and brakes all fail more often than tyres.
  • At the same age, hybrids fail least and diesels most. Electric cars fail more on tyres and least on brakes, consistent with their weight and regenerative braking.
